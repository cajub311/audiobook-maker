FAQ

Does the browser player work offline?

Yes — after your first visit, the launcher shell is cached by a service worker and the player uses your device's built-in speech synthesis.

Can I import an EPUB or PDF?

The browser player takes plain .txt or pasted text. For EPUB/PDF/URL ingestion and exported MP3/M4B files, use the Python app (Quick Start above).

Where are my drafts stored?

Entirely in your browser's localStorage. Clearing site data removes them.
