Tips

  • Works entirely in your browser. Audio is generated on-demand through Microsoft Edge neural voices.
  • Long chapters are auto-split on paragraph & sentence boundaries for cleaner pacing.
  • If generation is slow, lower the parallel request count or the input size.
  • For huge books (10k+ words) you may want to batch one chapter at a time.
