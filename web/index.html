Tips & how it works
  • Free by default: runs in your browser on Microsoft Edge neural voices — no keys, no cost, nothing leaves your device except the text being read.
  • Emotion: pick a narration style (or Auto) and the app adds natural feeling, pacing and pauses for you.
  • Long books: text is auto-split into chunks and rendered in parallel, then stitched into one MP3.
  • Multi-voice: turn it on to give quoted dialogue a different voice from the narrator.
  • Premium (optional): add an ElevenLabs key for studio voices — it falls back to free instantly if the quota runs out.
